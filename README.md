# Mapping app

I was looking for a place to live for a few months and wanted to find a place that was:
- Outside London
- Close to a train station
- Close to a supermarket (Waitrose)
- Close to an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty (AONB)
- With a few pubs nearby
- and close to a river

Within a bounding box and showing my travel times from Home (London) and a few close friends' homes.
